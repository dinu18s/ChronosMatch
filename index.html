⚠️ Market Microstructure Truth: Why Imbalance Is NOT a Standalone Trend Predictor
1. Fleeting Liquidity & Cancellations: Large resting bids can be cancelled milliseconds before an aggressive sell market order hits (commonly seen in algorithmic spoofing).
2. Hidden & Iceberg Orders: Institutional buyers often place iceberg limit orders that show only 10 shares displayed while hiding 5,000 shares behind the scenes.
3. Mean Reversion vs. Momentum: High positive imbalance can attract predatory aggressive selling or trigger rapid exhaustion rather than directional upward continuation.
